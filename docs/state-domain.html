(A) 2024 Measures by State and Domain

How each state performs within each Core Set domain in 2024. A state result is above the median when its rate is at least as good as the CMS median for that measure (CMS's “On Track” flag; direction-aware, so for lower-is-better measures a lower rate counts as above the median). Data: data.medicaid.gov, Quality theme.

Overview — top 10 and bottom 10 states

Score = measures the state reported and scored above the median ÷ all measures the state reported in the selected domain. To keep a state that reported one or two measures from topping the list on a small denominator, a state must report at least half of the selection's measure series to be ranked (). Ties are broken by number of measures reported.

      Domain composite — how the nation is doing by domain

      For each program × domain: how many states reported, how many distinct measures the domain holds, and the % of reporting states performing above the median — states where more than half of their reported measures in that domain are above the CMS median.

      How to read the %: the CMS median splits reporting states roughly in half on every single measure, so this composite cannot show absolute national improvement. What it shows is breadth. Above 50% means above-median results are spread across many states (most states are consistently above the median on the domain). Below 50% means they are concentrated in a smaller group of states while most states fall below on most measures. For change over time, see Trends 2022–24.
