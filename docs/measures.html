(B) 2024 Across all States by Measure Type and Domain

Every 2024 measure in the Child and Adult Core Sets, with every reporting state on one chart per measure, plus a cross-state composite. Pick a program, then a domain from the toolbar — only domains that contain measures for that program are shown. Data: data.medicaid.gov, Quality theme.

“Measure Type” in the source data is a direction flag, not a taxonomy — see methodology. Bars show each state’s reported rate for 2024, sorted ascending; colour encodes the rate on a domain scale. The red dashed line is the CMS median: states on the arrow side of it are underperforming (not on track), direction-aware. Where several rate series share one measure name, the follow-up period or sub-rate is added to the title in parentheses.

Composite state summary — On-Track share, 2024

For each state, the share of its reported 2024 measure rates where the state is on track (rate at least as good as the CMS median, direction-aware), split by program. Each rate definition counts separately (e.g. 7-day and 30-day follow-up are two rates). The composite pools all measures across all domains — it is not limited to the domain selected above.
